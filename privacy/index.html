Privacy Policy — Company Management Studio

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Company Management Studio is a private business operations application operated by BKR Tech SL. It is used only by authorized operators.

Google data

When an authorized operator connects Google services, the application requests only the access needed for the enabled workflow. This may include Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs. The application uses this access to read or process business files, spreadsheets and documents requested by the operator.

OAuth credentials and tokens are kept in private server-side configuration and are not displayed in the application or shared with third parties. Google data is not sold, rented or used for advertising. Access is limited to authorized operators and the services required to operate the application.

Other information

The application may process internal business records, work calendars, billing information and generated documents. It does not intentionally collect public-user profiles or unrelated personal information.

Retention and control

Records are retained only as needed for business operations, accounting and security. The Google account owner can revoke the application’s access from Google Account security settings. Requests about access or deletion can be sent to antalbokor@gmail.com.
